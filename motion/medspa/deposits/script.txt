How to collect payments and deposits through the platform.
The rule: deposits only come through your live booking links.
New patients book on your website, and pay the deposit right there.
New patient calls in? Don't book them in the app or the EMR. Text them the live booking link, or take their card and book them through that same link.
Existing patients book in the patient portal. No deposit.
Existing patient calls? Staff books it manually. No deposit.
Can't find your link? Check your website, ask the AI, or open the EMR: Calendars, then Booking links.
