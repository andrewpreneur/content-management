## Script

[0:00] If I were starting my GHL SaaS business today with everything I know now, here's the only five things I'd focus on.

[0:07] One: I'd learn the platform before I sold it. 60 days minimum using GHL on my own business before I onboarded a single paying client.

[0:14] Two: I'd start on the $297 plan, not $497. Upgrade the day two clients are covering the cost — not before.

[0:21] Three: I'd pick one vertical for my first five clients. Not "any business." One type — HVAC, dental, restaurants, whatever. Get exceptional at their specific GHL setup. Then expand.

[0:29] Four: I'd bundle the onboarding. A one-time setup fee in addition to the monthly. It covers your time and it signals that what you're delivering has value.

[0:36] Five: I'd use SasSuite for client support from day one. Don't try to answer every GHL question yourself. Let the support layer do its job so you can focus on growth.

[0:44] I've made most of these mistakes. I'm just telling you which ones I'd skip.
