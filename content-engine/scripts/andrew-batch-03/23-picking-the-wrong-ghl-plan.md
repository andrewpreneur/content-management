[0:07] The plans: Starter at $97, Unlimited at $297, and SaaS Pro at $497. Here's where I see people get it wrong. Beginners buying the $497 plan before they have a single client — because they want the SaaS features. I get the excitement. [0:20] Here's the rule I use: start at $97 if you're still learning the platform and have zero clients. Move to $297 when you're ready to serve clients and need unlimited sub-accounts.
