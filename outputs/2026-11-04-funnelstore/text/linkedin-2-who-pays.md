# LinkedIn 2 — who pays for it
_Angle: the buyer. Image: story frame 4 or a product-list page screenshot._

Most agency owners pitch features.

But clients don't buy features. They buy "I can finally sell my thing online."

HighLevel just made that pitch easy: you can now put a full store inside an existing funnel. One click builds the product list, product details, cart, checkout and thank-you pages, using the design that's already there.

Therefore the question isn't "how do I set it up." It's "who do I sell it to?"

Here's my short list:

→ Gyms and studios: day passes, class packs, merch.
→ Coaches: workbooks, templates, a paid mini-course.
→ Local shops: gift cards and best-sellers, without a separate Shopify.
→ Restaurants and cafés: merch, sauces, catering deposits.
→ Churches and nonprofits: donations and event tickets on one page.

Every one of these already has a funnel or a website. But most of them don't have a way to take money on it.

That gap is your offer.

Pick one niche from that list. Build one demo store. Show it on the sales call.

One feature. One niche. One demo.

That's how you start a SaaS on HighLevel.
