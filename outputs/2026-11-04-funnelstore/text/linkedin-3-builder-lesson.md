# LinkedIn 3 — building in public / the lesson
_Angle: what this says about how to grow. Image: Andrew on stage (photo library stage-1)._

I've watched hundreds of people try to sell HighLevel.

The ones who stall all do the same thing: they sell the whole platform. CRM, calendars, automations, AI, reputation, funnels, all at once.

But the buyer hears "complicated," therefore they say "let me think about it."

The ones who grow do the opposite. They pick one feature and make it the whole offer.

Today's example: HighLevel stores can now live inside any funnel. Click an element, choose the sections, import — and a one-page funnel turns into a five-page store.

That single change is a complete offer:
• Who it's for: any local business with a funnel and something to sell.
• What it fixes: "I want to sell online but I don't want another platform."
• What you deliver: the store, live, in their branding, this week.

But one feature only works if you can explain it in one sentence.

Here's mine: "I'll turn your funnel into a store this week."

If you can't say your offer in one sentence, you're selling too much.

What's your one feature?
