# Extended podcast segment — script (6–7 min, for "Get Real with Andrew George")
_Andrew reads this, or it's voiced with his ElevenLabs clone once the key works and a clone is confirmed. Same idea as the reel, deeper._

**[COLD OPEN]**
Stores can now be embedded directly inside existing funnels and websites in HighLevel. That sounds like a small product update. But I think it's one of the easiest offers you can sell this quarter, and I want to tell you why.

**[THE FEATURE — ~1 min]**
Here's what changed. Until now, if a client wanted to sell online inside HighLevel, you built a dedicated store from the Stores tab. Separate thing, separate setup. Most agencies skipped it and sent the client to Shopify.
Now you open a normal funnel, drop in a store element — a cart icon is the simplest — and HighLevel asks one question: which parts of this page do you want on every store page? Hero, gallery, testimonials, FAQ, footer. You tick the boxes, click Import, therefore your one-page funnel becomes a store: product list, product details, cart, checkout, thank you. Same design.

**[WHY IT MATTERS — ~1.5 min]**
But I don't care about the click. I care about what the click lets you say on a sales call.
Most people selling HighLevel pitch the platform. They walk a local business owner through the CRM and the automations and the calendars, and the owner hears one word: complicated. Therefore they stall.
Compare that to: "You already have a funnel. I'll turn it into a store this week." Nobody needs that explained.

**[WHO PAYS — ~1.5 min]**
Walk through it niche by niche. A gym: day passes, class packs, merch. A coach: a workbook, templates, a small paid course. A local shop: gift cards, best-sellers. A café: merch, sauces, catering deposits. A church or nonprofit: donations and event tickets.
Every one of them already has a page. But most can't take money on it. That gap is the offer.

**[WHAT TO CHARGE — ~1 min]**
Two parts. A one-time setup: the store live, in their branding, first products loaded, checkout tested. Then a monthly care plan: new products, seasonal promos, and — this is the HighLevel advantage — abandoned-cart and post-purchase follow-ups straight from the CRM. **[THE BIGGER LESSON — ~1 min]**
This is the whole idea behind what I teach. Don't sell all of HighLevel. Sell one feature. One feature, one niche, one demo.
But the moment it works, you hit the next wall: onboarding, educating and supporting ten, twenty, fifty clients. That's why I built SaaSSuite — one place for your clients.
