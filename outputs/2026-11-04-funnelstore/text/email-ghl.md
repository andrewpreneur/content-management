**Stores can now live inside an existing funnel or website.**

Before, if a client wanted to sell online inside HighLevel, you had to build a separate store from the Stores tab. Most people didn't bother. They sent the client to Shopify, therefore the client ended up with another login, another bill, and sales data that never touched the CRM.

Now it works like this:

1. Open the funnel (or website) in the builder.
2. Add any store element — a cart icon in the header is the easiest.
3. HighLevel pops up "Import theme." Tick the sections you want reused on every store page (hero, gallery, testimonials, FAQ, call to action, footer). Keep popups and fonts on.
4. Click Import.

That's it. Go back to the funnel steps and you'll see the new pages: **product list, product details, cart, checkout, thank you** — all in the design the funnel already had.
