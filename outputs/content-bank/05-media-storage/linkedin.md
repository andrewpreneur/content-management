Fun fact: HighLevel gives you unlimited media storage.

Every logo, photo, contract and video your clients upload. No cap.

But most agencies still pay for Google Drive or Dropbox per client. That's $10–$20 a month each, for storage they already have.

It's in every sub-account: Marketing → Media Storage. And it syncs with their funnels and automations.

Therefore this isn't a footnote. It's part of the pitch.

If you run a white-label SaaS, you're not selling software. You're giving clients infrastructure they'd otherwise pay for separately.
