# LinkedIn — Manual Actions
_Source: `content-engine/scripts/andrew-batch-03/24-manual-actions-most-underrated-ghl-tool.md` · feature: Manual Actions_

Everyone wants to automate everything.

But the deals that close usually need one human moment.

HighLevel's Manual Actions handle exactly that. Inside any workflow, add a step that creates a task for a specific team member: "Call and check in on the proposal." "Confirm the appointment." "Review this intake form." It lands in their queue at the right time.

It's your automation raising its hand and saying: this one needs a person.

Therefore your workflows don't have to do everything. They handle 90% of the process and hand the right 10% to the right person.

Add at least one manual action checkpoint to every pipeline stage.
