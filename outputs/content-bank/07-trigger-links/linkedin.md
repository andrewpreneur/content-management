# LinkedIn — Trigger links
_Source: `content-engine/scripts/andrew-batch-03/07-trigger-links-30-day-results.md` · feature: Trigger links_

30 days ago I added trigger links to three HighLevel email sequences.

A new-client welcome. A re-engagement campaign. A review request.

A trigger link fires a workflow the moment someone clicks it. In an email, a text, anywhere.

The re-engagement campaign fired 47 times.

That's 47 people tagged, moved to the right pipeline stage and dropped into the right follow-up. But nobody on my team touched them.

Zero manual tasks.

Therefore this is one feature I'd set up for every client: one link per intent, one workflow per link.
