# LinkedIn — Email warm-up
_Source: `content-engine/scripts/andrew-batch-03/15-email-warm-up-is-built-into-ghl.md` · feature: Email warm-up_

People launch their first HighLevel broadcast cold, and half of it lands in spam.

Then they blame the copy.

But it's not the copy. It's the sender reputation.

HighLevel has email warm-up built in. It slowly builds your reputation by trading low-volume, natural emails with other accounts before your real campaigns go out. Dedicated warm-up tools charge $15–$30 a month for this.

Sub-account settings → Email Services → warm-up toggle.

Therefore the rule is simple: turn it on at least two weeks before your first broadcast.
