# LinkedIn — 3 starter automations
_Source: `content-engine/scripts/andrew-batch-03/11-3-automations-for-every-new-client.md` · feature: 3 starter automations_

Before a new client gets their HighLevel login, I set up three automations.

1. Missed call text-back. A form fill or missed call gets a text in under 60 seconds. Therefore no lead dies waiting.

2. Review requests. 48 hours after service, an SMS with the Google review link. No reply in three days, a follow-up email. My clients average a full star improvement within 90 days.

3. Appointment reminders. Confirmed 24 hours out, reminded one hour before. No-shows drop close to zero.

But the point isn't the automations. It's that these three replace tools most clients already pay for.

Make sure all three run before you hand over the keys.
