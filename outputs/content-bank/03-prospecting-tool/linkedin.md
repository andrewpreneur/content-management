# LinkedIn — Prospecting Tool
_Source: `content-engine/scripts/andrew-batch-03/03-most-underutilized-ghl-feature.md` · feature: Prospecting Tool_

Most agencies walk into a first sales call blind.

They know the business name. Maybe the website. But they don't know the review score, where leads are leaking, or what's broken.

But HighLevel already has the fix, and most people have it switched off.

It's the Prospecting Tool. One search pulls business contact info, website health, social presence and Google review data.

Therefore your first call changes. You're not pitching from scratch. You're showing up knowing more than they do.

It lives on the Agency side: Settings → Business Services → Prospecting Tool.

Here's one feature you can sell: a "what's broken" report before the first call. Turn it on this week.
