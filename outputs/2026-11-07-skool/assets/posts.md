## 3. How to ask a question that gets answered (pinned guidelines) · graphic: `pinned-3-how-to-ask.jpg`

**Title:** How to ask a question that gets answered

When you get stuck, post your question or bring it into coaching.

When you ask a question, explain:
1. **What you want to accomplish**
2. **What you've tried**
3. **Where you're stuck**

That gives people enough context to help.

Also include what you're building and the decision you need help making.

Ask about the part of your workflow that's giving you trouble. Bring those questions into coaching too. You'll get more from a session when you arrive with something specific to work through.

And spend a little time looking at what other members are building. You may find an idea you can apply, someone working through the same challenge, or a question you already know how to answer.
