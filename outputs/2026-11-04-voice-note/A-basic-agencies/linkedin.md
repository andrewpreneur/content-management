# LinkedIn — Basic agencies get squeezed
_Source: `outputs/2026-11-04-voice-note/transcript.txt (6:44–11:30)` · feature: Basic agencies get squeezed_

I think basic marketing agencies have 6 to 12 months.

Not because they're bad. Because their clients are about to learn a new skill: using AI to use software.

If all you do is set up a CRM, build a website and manage it, the client will soon ask a fair question: why wouldn't I just ask Claude, or go to HighLevel directly?

But that doesn't mean agencies die. It means the job changes.

Therefore I'd focus on two things:

1. AI competency. The gap between what you can build with AI and what your client knows is your margin. Keep it wide.
2. Wrap it simpler. Most people will never go to HighLevel directly because it feels too complicated. Make it simple for them. That's the product.

The agencies that win won't sell software. They'll sell the gap.
