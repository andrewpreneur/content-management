# LinkedIn — The reason they don't go direct is you
_Source: `outputs/2026-11-04-voice-note/transcript.txt (14:00–15:05)` · feature: The reason they don't go direct is you_

Every client of yours could go straight to the software.

They could open their own HighLevel account. Build their own website. Ask ChatGPT to set it up.

But most won't.

Nine times out of ten, the reason is you.

They want to text a person. They want to say "Rob's got it covered" and stop thinking about it. Even when the fix is simple, they want to know a human is behind it.

Therefore the human touch isn't a nice extra on top of your AI offer. It's the reason the offer exists.

Build the system. But stay the person they call.
