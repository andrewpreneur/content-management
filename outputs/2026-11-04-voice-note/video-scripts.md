# Short-video scripts from the Nov 4 voice note (film these)
Camera A, talking head, 45–60 s. Say all 3 opening lines; each becomes its own main-feed post. Keep it conversational; these are your words from the voice note, tightened.

## A — "Basic agencies have 6–12 months" (pillar: AI you can sell)
**Openers (say all 3):**
1. "If your agency only sets up a CRM and a website, you have about a year."
2. "Your clients are about to ask you one question: why can't I just ask Claude?"
3. "Here's what's going to happen to marketing agencies in the next 12 months."
**Body:** Clients are learning to use AI to use software. **But** that doesn't kill agencies. It changes the job. **Therefore** two levers: (1) AI competency — the gap between what you can build and what they know is your margin. (2) Wrap it simpler — most people will never use HighLevel directly because it feels too complicated, so make it simple for them.
**Close:** "Don't sell software. Sell the gap. Comment AGENT."

## B — "Why clients don't go direct" (pillar: SaaS math & ops / building in public)
**Openers:**
1. "Every one of your clients could go straight to HighLevel. Most won't. Here's why."
2. "The reason your clients pay you isn't the software."
3. "'Rob's got it covered.' That sentence is your whole business."
**Body:** They could open their own account, build their own site, ask ChatGPT. **But** nine times out of ten they don't, because of you. They want to text a person. Even when the fix is simple, they want a human behind it. **Therefore** the human touch isn't an extra on top of your AI offer. It's the reason the offer exists.
**Close:** "Build the system. Stay the person they call. Comment AGENT."

## C — "Sell the AI setup, not the tool" (pillar: AI you can sell)
**Openers:**
1. "I asked a restaurant owner how they post on Facebook. 'Manually.'" *(only if true — otherwise use #2)*
2. "Logging your client into an AI tool doesn't work. Here's what does."
3. "AI setups aren't getting one-click anytime soon, and that's your opportunity."
**Body:** Show a business an AI system and they'll use it for a week, then stop. **But** not because it's hard. They don't know what it can do. Anything on a screen, an agent can probably handle. **But** setups stay complicated — logins, permissions, security. **Therefore** build the setup for one kind of business, teach what it can do, hand them a simple chat to run it, and duplicate it.
**Close:** "One system. One niche. Then the next client. Comment AGENT."
