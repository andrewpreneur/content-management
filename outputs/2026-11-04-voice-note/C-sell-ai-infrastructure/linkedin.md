# LinkedIn — Sell the AI setup, not the tool
_Source: `outputs/2026-11-04-voice-note/transcript.txt (8:43–9:45, 12:22–16:20)` · feature: Sell the AI setup, not the tool_

Walk into a local restaurant and ask how they post on Facebook.

"Manually. When I remember."

You could show them an AI system that does it. You could even log them into one. But they'd use it for a week and stop.

Not because it's hard. Because they don't know what it can do.

That's the real gap. Not "how to use AI." What AI can take off their plate. Anything they do on a screen, an agent can probably handle.

And the setup won't become one-click anytime soon. Security, logins and permissions keep it complicated.

Therefore the opportunity is: build the AI setup for one kind of business, teach them what it can do, give them a simple chat to run it, and duplicate it.

One system. One niche. Then the next client.
