What V8 does, and which example it came from

  1. Captions build a phrase word by word, each word popping in (Nick Saraev). Key words turn yellow italic serif.
  2. Hook header for the first sentence: plain line plus the punch phrase in a blue box that wipes in (Saraev).
  3. One big moment every ~3.5 s: small lead-in words, then the key word huge in colour with a punch-in zoom and a whoosh (gp_multimedia).
  4. Numbers count up ("30 years") with a pop (gp's 450,000 counter).
  5. Glass chips with an icon pop beside the speaker for key nouns (gp "Real Leads").
  6. Icon bursts on wins, money and referrals (gp's hearts).
  7. Handwritten name note with an arrow the first time a named guest talks (Diary of a CEO).
  8. Rack-focus blur on speaker changes, one black-and-white dip on the hardest negative line. No white flashes.
  9. Sound only on big moments, about one hit every 5 s, music ducked under the voice, -16 LUFS.
