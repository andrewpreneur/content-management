# LinkedIn 2 — deposits before withdrawals

A band director told me something years ago that still runs my sales process.

"You have to make deposits in people before you can make a withdrawal."

Every relationship has a give-to-ask ratio. But most people in sales ask too soon. They try to withdraw from an account they never paid into.

I call it perceived relationship equity. How much you've given before you ask.

Therefore the sales process is really a question: how fast can this person come to like and trust me?

Give value first. Make them feel heard. Explain exactly how you'd solve the problem.

Then ask.
