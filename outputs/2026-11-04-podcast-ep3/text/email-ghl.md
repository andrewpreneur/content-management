Hey {{contact.first_name}},

Picture the call. They're nodding, they like you, they say "we're ready, we just need a bit of time."

Then weeks go by and the deal fizzles out.

I lived in that loop when I started selling, so I wrote myself a document called **Positive to Payment**. I talked through it on the latest episode of Get Real. Here's the short version.

**People pay you when three things are true:**
1. **They understand they have a problem.** Ask questions until they say it out loud.
2. **They trust you to fix it.** "We can fix that" isn't enough. Show exactly how. That's why founders often outsell hired reps: they built the solution.
3. **They have a way to pay you right now.** A payment link or invoice ready before the call ends.

But even with all three, you need **commitment**: small actions they take with you that they don't take with anyone else (a lead magnet, a video, replying to your emails). If you're on HighLevel, engagement scoring on your contacts shows you who's actually leaning in.

Then **get paid live.** Send the link and stay on the call. If that feels like pressure, you haven't sold them enough yet.

And after they pay, **reinforce the decision.** A thank-you, something extra. Buyer's remorse is real.

Positive reactions mean nothing until they turn into payment.
