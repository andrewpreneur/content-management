# LinkedIn 1 — the framework
_From Get Real ep. 3 "Positive to Payment"._

The call goes great. They nod. They say "we're ready."

Then nothing. Weeks of silence.

I had this happen so often in my first years of sales that I wrote a document about it. I called it Positive to Payment.

Here's the short version. People pay you when three things are true:

1. They understand they have a problem.
2. They trust you to fix it.
3. They have a way to pay you, right now.

Most deals die on number three. Therefore the payment link should be ready before the call starts, not "I'll send it over."

A positive reaction means nothing until it turns into a payment.
