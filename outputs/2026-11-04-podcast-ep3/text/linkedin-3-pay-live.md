# LinkedIn 3 — get paid live

"If closing feels high pressure, you haven't sold them enough yet."

When someone's already in — they understand the problem, they trust you, they're happy — asking for payment isn't pressure. It's the next step.

So I do it live. "Here's the link. Let's get it taken care of." Then I stay on the call while they pay.

But the sale doesn't end there. Buyers get remorse. Therefore what you do in the first hour after they pay matters as much as the call itself: a thank-you, something extra, a reason to feel good about the decision.

Positive → payment → reinforcement. Skip the last step and you'll feel it in refunds.
