How every piece goes out

Made→Captioned→Your tap→Draft in Social Planner→Scheduled→Posted→Measured

You only do the tap. Approve, ask for a change with a one-line note, or skip. Agents read your taps from this page, then:

  • Captioner writes one caption per platform in your voice. It keeps one promise and one ask per post, with 3 hashtags on IG, none on Threads or X, and no em dashes or AI words.
  • Scheduler creates the post in GHL Social Planner as a draft at its planned slot. It stays inside the daily limits: 2–3 IG/FB, 1–2 LinkedIn, 2 Shorts, plus 1 story sequence.
  • Manual lane: Threads, X, Skool, email and IG stories with poll stickers come to you as ready-to-paste text.
  • Reporter pulls 7-day numbers each week and feeds the next plan.

Rules for agents live in the repo: posting/README.md, the queue tool tools/post_queue.py, and the skill posting-funnel. New content gets added to this page, never to a new one.
