# Teardown: the old creative guy's Meta ads (B002, B003, "_not approved")

Nov 7, 2026 · meta-ads worker · 39 statics + 5 SMS videos reviewed by eye (all 1080×1350).
Contact sheets are in `teardown/` (every file, labelled). No performance data was available, so "works / fails" below is a creative judgement against the Meta playbook (callout, one message, proof, native feel, message match) plus Andrew's own approve/reject signal.

## The one-line verdict
The **message** is strong and should be kept: *"I sell ONE GHL feature for $99/month"* and *"Nobody has to be convinced. They already WANT it."* The **execution** is the problem: 26 B003 statics are ~3 ideas in different costumes, there is zero proof anywhere, and the rejected set is what happens when an AI face drifts away from the real Andrew.

## What works (keep doing)
1. **The offer line.** "I sell ONE GHL feature for $99/month" is a specific, odd, believable claim with a number in it. It works as hook, footer and LP headline, so message match is easy. Kept in the new batch.
2. **"Nobody has to be convinced. They already WANT it."** The best line in the whole set: it names the buyer's real fear (selling is hard) and flips it. Kept.
3. **Real Andrew, clean editorial look (B003 V1–V12, B002 V2/V3).** Warm paper, bold serif headline, one short sub, a real photo of Andrew. Looks like a person talking, not a brand shouting. Andrew approved these. The new `editorial` template copies this look.
4. **Native formats.** The iMessage videos (B002 V7–V12), Notes app (V20) and browser/phone frames (V26) feel like content, not ads, and they force a curiosity gap ("Which one?"). Kept and extended (Notes, tweet, Google, Reddit, sticky note).
5. **Curiosity → link card structure in the SMS videos.** Hook, objection/question from "the viewer", answer is the YouTube card. 8–10 s, readable on mute. This is the template for the new motion ads V01–V04.
6. **The whiteboard 3 steps (B002 V3):** "Turn off everything else / One page. One video. / They build. I get paid." A mechanism in 12 words. Reused as sticky note C11d and list C16b.

## What fails (stop doing)
1. **No proof, anywhere.** 44 creatives and not one number, result, customer or screenshot of the product. The playbook says lead with proof; this batch can only ever win on curiosity. Fix: real testimonial quotes (Rob, Brentney, Ben, Jessica) and real GHL UI are now in the batch.
2. **Fake diversity.** B003 V13–V26 are 14 skins of one sentence. Since Andromeda, near-duplicates are treated as one concept and get punished with higher CPMs; you learn nothing from testing them against each other. B003 V1–V12 are 12 headlines on one identical photo: same problem. Fix: 16 genuinely different concepts (pain, outcome, proof, math, us-vs-them, feature, founder, contrarian, persona callouts, native), 4–5 variants each.
3. **AI Andrew who isn't Andrew (S01, S03, S04, C06).** The S-series are AI remakes of approved ads, but the face is older, broader and more muscular; C06 is a bodybuilder. Andrew rejected all of them, rightly: an AI face passing as the real founder is exactly what 2026 Meta guidance says to avoid ("ultra-raw or unmistakably AI, never AI posing as human") and it breaks trust the moment someone watches the real VSL. Rule kept: only real frames of Andrew are used in the new batch (pulled from the VSL shoot).
4. **Cartoon / meme costume (B002 V4 manga, C02 superhero, C05 virgin vs chad, C07 "prove me wrong").** Funny once, but they call out "meme followers", not GHL agency owners, so Meta finds the wrong people. The callout is the targeting now.
5. **Vague product.** "Vibe code", "AI builder", "a tool" (B003 V1–V12): the viewer never learns it is a HighLevel feature they can white-label. Some read like crypto ads. Fix: say HighLevel, say "your name on it", show the UI.
6. **The SaaSSuite half of the offer is missing.** Nothing about onboarding, training videos or support, which is what Rob and Brentney actually praise. New concepts C03, C07, C04 and part of C05/C08/C10/C15/C16 cover it (LP-B).
7. **One CTA path, no persona.** Everything points to one YouTube video and speaks to "you". New batch adds persona callouts (GHL affiliates, agency owners, coaches, web designers) and two landing pages so each angle can get its own LP.
8. **Small craft issues.** Tiny sub-text (unreadable in feed at 4:5 when shrunk), 3D clip-art people (V16) and a robot (V22) that look like stock, headlines broken over 4–5 lines in some skins.

## Andrew's approve/reject pattern (what to infer)
- Approved: real face, clean type, one sentence, the offer line, native UIs.
- Rejected: anything where "Andrew" is AI-altered or exaggerated, and loud meme/comic gimmicks.
- So: authenticity > gimmick. The new batch follows that.

## How the new batch applies this
See `README.md`. 65 statics in 16 concepts (each 4–5 variants for one ABO ad set), 20 of them also in 9:16, 18 video ads (12 motion built from the static templates, 6 real-footage: 3 Andrew VSL cuts, 3 customer testimonials). Every face is real. Every quote is a word-for-word transcription.
