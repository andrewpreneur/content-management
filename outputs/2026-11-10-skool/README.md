### 1. About page (~75 s)
If you're trying to build a business with AI right now, you're not short on tools. GoHighLevel, Claude, app builders, agents, and a new one every week.

But the tools don't tell you what to build, who to sell it to, or how to actually get paid. So most people stay stuck testing things.

That's why I built this community. Here's what you get inside.

First, you get access to me. Bring your offer, your build, or your question, and I'll help you work through it.

Second, the training. Step by step on GoHighLevel, building with AI, packaging a SaaS offer, and getting clients.

Third, calls with other successful entrepreneurs. People who've already built what you're working on, sharing what actually worked.

And fourth, first access to the new software and systems I'm building, before anyone else gets them.

So whether you're starting with an idea, already run an agency, or just want AI to make your business easier to run, come inside, find your starting point, and start building.

### 2. Onboarding (~60 s)
Welcome in. Glad you're here.

You've got four things in here: me, the training, calls with other entrepreneurs, and first access to what I'm building.

But the people who get results don't try to use everything on day one. They pick one goal.

So here are your first three steps.

One: introduce yourself. Post what you do, what you want to build, and your goal for this week.

Two: open the Classroom and start with the training that matches that goal. New? Start with foundations. Have an idea? Go to building. Have an offer? Go to getting clients.

Three: check the calendar and get on the next call. Bring one specific question.

And keep an eye on announcements. That's where new software and systems drop first.

Go make your intro post, and I'll see you inside.
