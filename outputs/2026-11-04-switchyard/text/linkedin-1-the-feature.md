# LinkedIn 1 — the feature (novel-style)
_Image: carousel slide 1 or the split-screen still from the master reel._

HighLevel just changed the left-hand menu.

That sounds like a design update. But it's bigger than that.

In the new UI, there's a small "Edit" button at the bottom of every sub-account's navigation. Click it, and you can reorder the tabs, hide whole sections, add quick links, rename categories and swap icons.

Therefore the question every agency owner has asked for years finally has an answer: "How do I stop my clients from getting lost in HighLevel?"

You hide what they don't need.

A dentist doesn't need Affiliate Manager. A roofer doesn't need Creator Studio. But both need Conversations front and center.

Then you save the layout, and apply it to every other sub-account in that niche. One click each.

That's not a menu setting.

That's a product: a version of HighLevel built for one kind of business.

Don't sell all of HighLevel. Sell one feature.
