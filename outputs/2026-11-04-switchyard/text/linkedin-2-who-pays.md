# LinkedIn 2 — who pays for it

Most HighLevel clients don't churn because the software is bad.

They churn because they log in, see 20 tabs, and never come back.

But HighLevel's new navigation lets you fix that per client. Hide sections. Reorder tabs. Add one-click links to the three screens they actually use. Save it as a layout and apply it to other sub-accounts.

Therefore you can sell something most agencies can't: a HighLevel that looks like it was built for one industry.

Who pays for that?

→ Agencies with 10+ sub-accounts in one niche (med spas, roofers, gyms) who are tired of "where do I find…" tickets.
→ SaaS builders selling a white-label CRM who need it to feel simple on day one.
→ Coaches and consultants who give clients a portal and want only 4 tabs visible.

The setup takes minutes. But the outcome — fewer support tickets, clients who actually log in — is what they're paying for.

One feature. One niche. One layout.
