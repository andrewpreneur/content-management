Hey {{contact.first_name}},

If you've ever had a client say "I can't find anything in here," this one's for you.

HighLevel rolled out a new UI you can turn on per sub-account. Most of it is navigation: the account switcher moved to a collapsed left nav, all the AI tools now sit under one AI tab, and the old row of buttons along the top became a dropdown.

But one part matters more than the rest: **you can now edit the left-hand navigation for each sub-account.**

Here's what the Edit button lets you do:

1. **Reorder tabs** — put Conversations or AI first.
2. **Hide whole sections** — a client who'll never use the CRM pipeline doesn't have to see it.
3. **Add quick links** — make "Conversations" its own one-click item.
4. **Customize categories** — rename them, change icons, move items between sections.
5. **Save the layout and apply it** to other sub-accounts from the Layout Selector.

**But here's why I'm emailing you about it.**

Clients don't leave HighLevel because it can't do enough. They leave because it does too much, and they get lost. Therefore the agency that shows up with a clean, niche-specific layout wins the "this is easy" moment on day one.

A simple way to package it:
- **Niche layout setup:** design one layout for an industry (say, med spas), apply it to every client in that niche.
- **Onboarding call:** walk the client through their 4–5 tabs. That's it.
- **Ongoing:** new client in that niche? Apply the layout in one click.

This is exactly what SaaSSuite is built around: one place for your clients, so they onboard, learn and get support without drowning in features.
