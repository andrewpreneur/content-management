# LinkedIn 3 — the lesson
_Image: Andrew on stage (photo library stage-2)._

"HighLevel is too complicated" is the most common objection I hear.

For years the honest answer was "yes, it is." You could train clients, record Looms, build help docs. But they still logged into a screen with every feature on it.

The new HighLevel UI changes the answer.

You can now decide what each client sees. Hide the sections they'll never use, pin the ones they need, and save that as a layout you apply across sub-accounts.

Therefore "too complicated" becomes a design choice you control.

But here's the lesson I'd take from it, beyond the feature: simplicity is the product.

Clients don't pay for more features. They pay for fewer decisions.

That's the whole idea behind SaaSSuite — one place for your clients to onboard, learn and get support. The new layouts are the same idea inside HighLevel itself.

What would you hide first?
