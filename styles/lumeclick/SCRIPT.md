# How LumeClick videos talk
- Talk like a contractor's friend who knows marketing: short, plain sentences. No jargon without the plain words next to it ("SEO-optimized website, made to turn visitors into phone calls").
- Shape: the pain in one line (missed calls / not found) → why (they call whoever shows up first) → the offer with the price → what we do, one scene per thing → the growth path → proof (a real review, a real case study with numbers) → no contracts + free audit + lumeclick.com.
- One idea per line, 8–20 words. Numbers said the way people say them ("ninety-nine dollars a month", "two or three a day").
- Pace: about 2.7 words a second (ElevenLabs speed 1.08), 0.5 s between lines. 45–55 s for a 9-scene video.
- Example (approved Nov 24): example/index.html + lines.js.
