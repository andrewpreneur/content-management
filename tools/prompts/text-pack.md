# Text-pack writer prompts (multiplier step 6)
Give the model: `docs/brand.md`, Second Brain "Brand voice", the source `transcript.txt`, and the run's CTA keyword. One prompt per output type. Every output obeys:
- Voice: plain, direct, founder energy, visual teacher. Short sentences. "HighLevel", "SaaSSuite".
- **But/Therefore:** beats connect with *but* (turn) or *therefore* (result), never "and then".
- One feature. Who pays. What to charge. How to set it up.
- **Never invent** client stories, numbers, prices or results. Use `[Andrew: real price/example]` placeholders, or frame as a scenario ("Picture a gym owner…").
- Same CTA keyword everywhere in the run.

## LinkedIn (×3, novel-style, ~180–250 words)
"Write 3 LinkedIn posts from this transcript. Novel-style: one-line paragraphs, scene first, lesson last. Angles: (1) the feature — what changed; (2) who pays — buyer list; (3) the lesson — one feature beats the whole platform. End with a question or the keyword CTA. No hashtags, no emojis."

## Threads (×3, ≤280 chars) and X thread (6–8 posts)
"3 Threads posts: (1) the news in 4 lines, (2) the pitch line agencies should use, (3) who'd buy it. Then an X thread: hook 1/, feature, steps, result, the offer, who buys, close 'Sell one feature.'"

## Long email (GHL)
"3 subject lines + preview text. Body: what changed → 4 numbered steps → *but* the setup isn't the value, the offer is → simple package (setup + monthly) → SaaSSuite tie-in (onboard/educate/support) → reply KEYWORD CTA. Merge field {{contact.first_name}}."

## Substack / community essay (600–900 words)
"Rewrite the email as an essay: the wall agencies hit → the change → why it matters (3 short scenarios) → 'one feature is a business' framework → SaaSSuite → invitation."

## YouTube long-form (Sell This Feature format)
"4 titles, 3 thumbnail concepts, timestamped outline with the 4 beats: feature → who pays → what to charge → build it live; cold open with the strongest real line from the transcript."

## Reel captions
"One caption per video version (master, each re-edit, trial reels). ≤3 short lines + CTA + ≤5 hashtags."

## Podcast extended segment (5–7 min script)
"Cold open with the transcript's first line, then: feature, why it matters, who pays, what to charge, the bigger lesson, CTA. Mark price placeholders."
