# Instagram Stories — sales and nurture playbook

For @andrewpreneurship (and @aiandrewpreneurship with the AI lens). Read `docs/brand.md` first.
Stories exist to turn people who already follow Andrew into **DMs, email subscribers and SaaSSuite trials**. Reels bring new people in; Stories do the warming and the asking.
Researched Nov 4, 2026. Every source is listed at the bottom. Anything we couldn't verify is marked **(unverified)**.

---

## 1. Proven sequences, and how each creator's version differs

### The core sequence we use
**Hook → authority/context → proof → value → engagement sticker → keyword CTA**

| Frame | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Hook | Viewers tap forward fast. Say what the story is about in the first frame or they leave (McCall: "set expectations", "imagine if…"). |
| Authority / context | It's Andrew's own feature pick, so it's his take, not generic news. Darma's close sequence starts with context before the offer. |
| Proof | A real screen recording, result or testimonial. McCall's "opt-in sandwich" puts the testimonial between the offer and the ask. |
| Value | Give before asking (Hormozi's give/ask ratio; Locklin's "the key to selling is SERVING"). For Andrew this is who pays for it and what to charge. |
| Engagement sticker | Easy taps first (poll), harder asks later (DM). People who answer a poll are more likely to DM (McCall). A "send me the link" poll option gives a list to DM by hand (Darma). |
| Keyword CTA | One action only: "DM me STORE." The DM starts a real conversation and the link is clickable there. McCall calls link-only CTAs "too passive." |

### Creator comparison
| Creator | Documented framework (from their own talks or show notes) | How it differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Tyler J. McCall** (Follower to Fan Society) | Every story needs a beginning, middle and end. **Opt-in sandwich**: introduce the freebie → testimonial → "send me a DM". Send links by DM, then follow up a day later. 10–12 frames a day at most, 5+ days a week. Reported 89 waitlist signups in 24 h and $2,400 in 12 h from a Stories-only sale. | The most "DM over link" of the group. Measures **retention** and **DM count**, not views. |
| **Elise Darma** (Story Seasons Method) | Four seasons: Visibility → Engagement → Leads → Sales. Sales needs at least 3 frames: context → offer (deadline or discount) → "reply with questions". A **quiz sticker lists objections**, then she DMs each answer. Hot leads go on Close Friends. A "Send me the link" poll option. | The most structured about the funnel stage. Uses stickers to surface objections. |
| **Ashly Locklin** (podcast ep. 15) | Question or poll opener → interaction → value (mini-training, tips) → pitch (pain → solution, said with confidence) → one clear CTA (DM, bio link or email). Captions on everything. About 70% business, 30% personal. | Leads with value and personality on camera. Treats design-app text slides as "occasional". |
| **Alex Hormozi** ($100M Leads, secondary summaries) | Content unit = **Hook → Retain → Reward**. "Give until they ask": several give pieces for each ask (summaries say ~3.5–4:1, **ratio unverified against the book**). | Not a Stories framework. Use it to set the give/ask ratio across the week and to make sure each frame pays off its hook. |
| **Jasmine Star** (SME podcast ep. 225) | About 3 value posts per promo. Frame the CTA around the **customer's problem**, not the product. Walking followers through the buying journey raises sales. She says high-ticket is "extraordinarily difficult to sell on Instagram." | Feed-era advice, but the problem-first CTA rule carries over to Stories. |
| **Nik Setting** (@niksetting, ~191K) | His site lists positioning → profile-funnel ads + automated outbound → a "content ecosystem" feeding "3 types of conversion funnels" → appointment setting with follow-up sequences. | **(Unverified)** We found no public Stories-specific framework from him. His YouTube was blocked from this session. Trustpilot shows 6 reviews, all 1-star and unverified. Useful lesson: Stories and content exist to feed a **setter/DM follow-up** process. |
| **Shelby Sapp** (She Sells Academy) | Objections are buying signals ("Objections are opportunities"). Reframe price vs. cost. Skip small talk. Follow up relentlessly ("like a psychopath with your leads", Inc., via search snippet). | **(Unverified)** We found no documented Stories framework. Her lessons apply to the **DM conversation after the keyword**. |

**Takeaway for Andrew.** The sequence is a feeder. The sale happens in the DM (McCall, Darma, Setting), so a keyword DM to GHL to a human or AI follow-up is the real product. Andrew sells low-ticket trials, not $10K coaching, so DM volume can be automated (section 4).

## 2. Frame-by-frame templates

### Template A — "Sell This Feature" drop (7 frames, Tue + Thu)
| # | Purpose | Visual | On-screen text (max) | Sticker | Example copy (Andrew's voice) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Real photo/selfie video, phone, desk | 8 words | none | "HighLevel just shipped something you can sell." |
| 2 | Authority | Real photo or 3-s talking clip | 10 | none | "Here's one feature you can sell this week…" |
| 3 | Proof (demo) | **Screen recording** (real UI) | 12 | none | "Watch. One click. Done." |
| 4 | Value: who pays | Real photo or AI lifestyle image of the buyer (not Andrew) | 15 | poll | "Who buys it: gyms, coaches, local shops. Would yours?" Yes / Show me |
| 5 | Value: what to charge | Text card on brand background | 12 | slider or none | "Charge setup + monthly. Don't give it away." |
| 6 | Proof (people) | **Real** client DM, comment or community screenshot | 12 | none | "This is why I keep saying: sell one feature." |
| 7 | CTA | Real photo, Andrew pointing down at the reply bar | 10 | none (keyword in text) | "DM me STORE. I'll send the setup." |

### Template B — Opt-in sandwich for The One Feature Playbook (4 frames, Wed)
1. **Problem** (real photo, ≤10 words): "Most agencies sell 40 features. Sell one." With a poll: "Selling SaaS yet?" Yes / Not yet.
2. **Offer** (mockup of the Playbook PDF + GHL snapshot, ≤12 words): "I wrote down every feature I'd sell first."
3. **Proof** (real reply or testimonial screenshot, ≤10 words of overlay).
4. **CTA** (real photo, ≤8 words): "DM me FEATURE for the Playbook."

### Template C — Close (5 frames, Fri)
1. **Context** (real photo): "This week I showed you 2 features to sell."
2. **Offer** (screen recording of SaaSSuite): "SaaSSuite runs them at scale. Free trial."
3. **Objection quiz** (Darma): "What's stopping you?" Options: Price / Time / Don't know GHL / Nothing. DM each answer.
4. **Proof** (real customer result only, never AI-made).
5. **CTA**: "DM me SUITE" plus a **link sticker** to the trial (one of the two days a link is OK).

### Template D — Warm-up (2–4 frames, Mon/Sat)
Building-in-public phone capture (real), then a poll or question box ("What feature should I break down Tuesday?"). No ask. These are the "give" days.

**Visual rules**
- Proof frames are always real: real screen recordings, real DMs, real results.
- An AI lifestyle image may show the *buyer* or a *scene* only. It must never be a fake result, testimonial or UI.
- Never an AI talking head of Andrew (`docs/ai-video-rules.md`). Andrew on camera = real phone footage.

## 3. Text styles (1080 × 1920 canvas)

**Safe zones**
- Top 250 px: progress bar, avatar and name cover it.
- Bottom 340 px: reply bar and CTA cover it. Meta's ad guidance is about 14% / 20%. Some guides say ~250 px is enough for organic Stories; we design to 340 so one template works for organic and ads.
- Sides: 64 px each.
- Usable box: **x 64–1016, y 250–1580.**

**Placement**
- Headline in the upper third (y ≈ 300–700).
- Stickers in the lower-middle band (y ≈ 1100–1500), never over the face or the UI being demoed.
- The CTA line sits right above 1580 so the eye drops straight onto the reply bar.

**Font sizes (at 1080 wide)**
| Element | Size | Weight | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | 72–88 px | Bold or black | 2 lines max, ≤8 words |
| Body | 44–52 px | Medium | 3 lines max |
| Label/caption | 36–40 px | Medium | Never smaller than 36 px |
| Keyword | 96–120 px | Black, all caps | Accent color, alone on its line (e.g. **STORE**) |

**Contrast boxes**
- Text over photo or video sits on a solid box: black at 75–85% opacity or the brand accent, white text, 24–32 px padding, 16 px radius.
- Aim for WCAG AA (4.5:1) or better. Never put plain white text on a busy screen recording.
- Burn captions into every talking clip (Locklin: many viewers watch on mute).

**Words per frame**
- Hook: ≤8. Normal: ≤15. Hard cap: 20.
- A photo frame shows for only a few seconds (sources cite 5–8 s, and it varies by app version). If it takes longer than about 3 s to read, split it.
- One idea per frame.

## 4. Cadence and the DM → GHL loop

**Volume:** 4–8 frames per day, never more than 12 (McCall: past ~12 the progress bar turns to dots and viewers drop). Post 6 days a week. Keep the week at about 3 give frames per ask (Hormozi, Jasmine Star).

| Day | Sequence | Keyword |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | D warm-up: building in public + "which feature Tuesday?" poll | — |
| Tue | A: Sell This Feature drop (same feature as the week's long-form ep.) | feature-specific (e.g. STORE) |
| Wed | B: opt-in sandwich → Playbook | FEATURE |
| Thu | A: HighLevel news / 2nd feature | feature-specific or AI |
| Fri | C: close → SaaSSuite trial | SUITE |
| Sat | D: personal or team, light | — |
| Sun | off, or a 1-frame recap of the week's best reel | — |

**Feeding DMs and the GHL list**
1. A viewer replies to the story or DMs the keyword (e.g. `STORE`). Story replies land in Instagram DMs, so the same keyword automation catches both.
2. **GHL workflow.** Trigger: Instagram DM / "Customer Replied" on the Instagram channel, message contains `STORE`. GHL also has a separate comment-keyword trigger for reels and posts.
   **(Unverified)** Trigger names come from third-party GHL guides. The main session should confirm in the sub-account. We found no GHL-specific story-reply trigger; ManyChat has one if GHL doesn't catch story replies.
3. Workflow actions:
   - Instant DM: "Here it is → [link]. What's the best email so I can send the snapshot too?"
   - Create or update the contact. Tag `ig-story`, `kw-STORE`, `pillar-sell-this-feature`.
   - Add to the "IG Story Leads" pipeline.
   - On email capture, add the contact to the email nurture.
4. **Follow-up** (McCall, Sapp): if there's no click or no email after 24 h, send one human-sounding nudge DM. Poll voters who picked "Show me" get a DM too (Darma).
   **(Unverified)** Stay inside Meta's messaging window after the user's last message; confirm the current rule before automating day-2 messages.
5. **Retargeting:** add hot leads (asked a question, answered the objection quiz) to Close Friends for Friday's close (Darma).
6. Run one keyword per CTA. Track each keyword's DM → email → trial counts in GHL. That is the attribution.

## 5. What to measure (weekly)
- **Replies + keyword DMs per 1,000 story views** (main KPI), plus DM → email → SaaSSuite-trial conversion per keyword in GHL.
- **Sticker taps** (poll/quiz/question responses) and **link-sticker clicks**, by frame and sequence type.
- **Completion and exit by frame** (completion = (impressions − exits) / impressions). A frame that loses more than ~10 points vs. the frame before it gets rewritten. The ~70% average completion is one vendor's figure **(unverified)**; benchmark against our own past stories.
