## How to read this

- **Method:** web search only. Direct page fetches (youtube.com, support.google.com, tubefilter.com, thestackinsiders.com) were **blocked by the session's network policy**, so no channel page was opened directly. Handles and numbers come from search snippets, third-party trackers (CreatorDB, vidIQ, Socialcounts, Social Blade) and podcast/show notes.
- **Numbers:** a figure is listed only if a source showed it, with the source and date. Third-party trackers lag and disagree. Anything not seen in a source is marked **unverified**.
- **Handles:** "(seen)" means a source showed the handle. "(likely)" means it's the expected handle but no source here confirmed it. Check it before tagging or scraping.
- **Hooks:** "hook patterns" are real video and episode titles that came up in search results in 2025–2026. They are not paraphrases.

HighLevel pays clippers, so the feed will fill with official-footage clips. Andrew wins by being the **face that explains and implements** the feature, not by recycling HighLevel's footage. ## Multi-language / translated channels: what they tell us

| Example | Approach | Result (source) |
|---|---|---|
| **MrBeast** | Started with **separate channels** (MrBeast en Español, Portuguese), then moved to **multi-language audio tracks** on the main channel (11 languages in 2023, 12 on some videos) | Spanish channel reportedly up to 100M views/video and Portuguese 5–10M/video before the switch ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/multi-language-audio-mrbeast-interview/), [Tubefilter 2023](https://tubefilter.com/2023/02/23/youtube-multi-language-tracks-tool-mrbeast-interview/)) |
| **Jamie Oliver** | Dubbed audio tracks on the main channel | Viewership reportedly **tripled** after adding dubs (YouTube auto-dubbing coverage, [YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-auto-dubbing-explained/)) |
| **YouTube aggregate** | Multi-language audio | Creators uploading MLA tracks saw **25%+ of watch time** from non-primary-language views (YouTube PM, via [Yahoo/YouTube expansion coverage](https://tech.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/youtube-expands-multi-language-audio-140103366.html)); 2023 pilot was 15% |
| **Linguana localized channels** | Separate localized channels run by a vendor | 100M+ views, 500K subs, "tens of thousands USD/month" (**vendor claim**, [Tubefilter Jan 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/14/youtube-content-localization-data-bassfishingproductions-linguana/amp/)) |
| **GHL niche already** | Someone is publishing **Spanish and Italian** "GoHighLevel tutorial for beginners 2026" videos ([ES](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S-NK-9VkaJ8), [IT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73Ac3gLzwn8)). Ecosire publishes GHL agency guides in ES/DE/JA/AR/HI | GHL demand outside English is real and lightly served |

**Takeaways for Andrew:**
1. **Use YouTube multi-language audio on the main channel first, not separate channels.** Views consolidate on one channel, there are no extra duplicate-content risks, and auto-dubbing now covers 27 languages (Gemini-powered, opt-out/review available) ([coverage](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/youtube-expands-auto-dubbing-to-27-languages-adds-more-natural-and-expressive-voices-88923.htm)). Upload a reviewed ElevenLabs dub as a custom track when quality matters.
2. **On IG/FB, use Meta AI's native translation** (voice clone plus optional lip-sync): EN/ES/PT/HI, then Indian languages (Jan 2026), then JA/KO/FR/DE/IT reported July 2026 ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/discover-reels-around-world-meta-ai-translation/), [relevantaudience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/instagram-reels-ai-translations-japanese-korean-french-german-italian/)). You get one post and many languages with no re-uploads.
3. **Separate language accounts only after one language proves out** (e.g., "@andrewpreneurship.es" for LATAM/Spain agencies). They need localized hooks, thumbnails and titles, not just dubbed audio. Start with **Spanish, then Portuguese (Brazil)**: big agency markets with lighter GHL competition. CPMs are lower (Brazil $1.50–$4, India $0.80–$2.50 per vendor guides), but Andrew sells SaaS, so reach matters more than AdSense.
4. **Dubbed content still counts as Andrew's own original content.** The real voice is cloned and the real face stays on screen, which matches the "no fully AI avatar" rule. Disclose AI dubbing where the platform labels it (Meta and YouTube auto-label).
