_For Andrew George's content engine. Goal context: 100 posts/day by day 60 and 1,000/day by day 90, using AI-assisted repurposing of **real** Andrew footage. No fully AI avatar videos._

**Method note:** web search only. Direct fetches of youtube.com, support.google.com, tubefilter.com and thestackinsiders.com were **blocked by the network policy**, so policy text comes from news and secondary coverage and is cited as such. Vendor blogs are flagged as vendor claims.

## TL;DR (read this if nothing else)

1. **The 100/day math is a repurposing pipeline, not a filming schedule.** Sabrina Ramonov (Blotato founder) says she ships **250+ pieces/week solo** with Claude Code + Blotato (self-reported, [press](https://kdhnews.com/online_features/press_releases/blotato-founder-reveals-the-social-media-funnel-behind-4-million-views/article_8d55227d-f038-5c7c-939b-df27e21da3bd.html)). Hormozi's team went from 7 to 80 pieces/week through repurposing ([Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)).
2. **The biggest risk is "unoriginal content", not AI.** Since **Apr 30 2026**, Instagram removes accounts that mostly post unoriginal content from recommendations, now including **photos and carousels**, not just Reels. Watermarks, speed changes and credited screenshots **do not** count as original ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/), [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/05/04/reposted-photos-and-carousels-now-face-reach-penalties-on-instagram/)).
3. **Don't run the same clip on multiple accounts.** 1,000/day has to mean **more distinct edits** (different hook, cut, layout, language, platform-native format) across Andrew's own accounts, not copies.
4. **Translation is the cheapest legit multiplier.** YouTube auto-dub covers 27 languages. Meta AI translates Reels with your cloned voice plus optional lip-sync. YouTube reports **25%+ of watch time** from non-primary languages for creators using multi-language audio.
5. **Disclosing AI does not reduce reach on YouTube** ("won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money", per YouTube documentation quoted by [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-shifts-generative-ai-labels-to-spots-viewers-will-actually-see/)). Label realistic synthetic b-roll, and don't hide it.

### 1e. Caption / hook variant testing

- **Instagram Trial Reels** show a reel to **non-followers only**, then you choose to share it to followers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers)). Method used by creators: make 2–3 versions that differ **only in the first 1–3s hook**, post as Trial Reels ~24h apart, keep the one with the best completion/sends ([Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/what-is-a-reel-trial-and-how-does-it-work), [Publer](https://publer.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-guide/)). The 24h spacing is a blog recommendation, not an IG rule.
- **Ask for distinct hook patterns, not "10 hooks".** Unstructured requests give near-duplicates ([AItomation Academy](https://www.aitomationacademy.com/blog/claude-for-creators)). One skill generates 10 hooks across 8 labeled frameworks.
- **YouTube "Test & Compare"** (thumbnails/titles A/B in Studio) for long-form.
- Trial Reel variants must be **re-edited**, not re-uploads. One vendor reports re-uploading an existing feed Reel gets suppressed (unverified).

## 2. Editing styles trending for short-form education

## 3. Prompts & workflows worth stealing

These are our adaptations of the cited patterns. Each is written as a Claude skill.

### 3a. Clip-mining skill (from transcript with timestamps)
```
ROLE: Short-form editor for Andrew George (HighLevel SaaS educator).
INPUT: timestamped transcript of a long-form video.
TASK: Return 15 clip candidates as a table:
start | end | 1-line idea | hook pattern (one of: warning, contrarian, number/authority,
how-to-step, mistake, before/after, myth-bust, question) | standalone? (y/n) | CTA fit
RULES: 25–60s; must make sense without context; prefer moments with a screen demo;
no two clips may share the same hook pattern AND idea; rank by "would an agency owner send this to a partner?"
```
_Pattern sources: Hormozi "highest-energy 30–90s moments"; distinct-pattern hooks ([AItomation](https://www.aitomationacademy.com/blog/claude-for-creators))._

### 3b. Hook-variant skill (for Trial Reels)
```
Given this clip script, write 6 opening lines (≤12 words, first 2 seconds), ONE per framework:
1 Warning ("Don't X until…")  2 Contrarian ("Everyone says X. Wrong.")  3 Authority-by-number (real numbers only: {facts})
4 Outcome ("This setting got my client…")  5 Mistake ("You're losing $ because…")  6 Curiosity gap.
Label each. Also give on-screen text (≤6 words) that differs from the spoken hook.
```
_Then: 2–3 Trial Reels, each a re-edit with its own hook, ~24h apart. Promote the winner._

### 3c. Platform-native rewrite skill (one idea → 7 native assets)
```
From this clip transcript produce:
- IG Reel caption (≤150 chars + 1 keyword line, no hashtag spam)
- TikTok caption + pinned comment CTA ("Comment SAAS for the snapshot")
- YT Shorts title (≤60 chars, keyword first) + description line
- LinkedIn text post (realandrewgeorge voice: first-person lesson, 120–200 words, no emojis)
- Threads post (1 punchy take ≤280 chars) + 1 reply-bait question
- FB post (story framing)
- Carousel outline (7 slides: hook, 5 steps, CTA)
Never reuse identical sentences across platforms.
```
_Forcing a different sentence per platform also lowers the text-duplicate risk._

### 3d. Pipeline (Claude Code + Blotato), adapted from [mejba.me](https://www.mejba.me/blog/claude-code-blotato-content-repurposing)
1. Drop recording → transcript (Whisper/Descript).
2. `clip-mine` skill → clip list → editor/Opus cuts **distinct** edits (layout, hook, caption style vary).
3. `hook-variants` + `platform-native` skills → copy JSON per platform.
4. Images <4MB → Blotato API **as drafts** (never auto-publish).
5. Human approve (batch, ~20 min/day) → schedule.
6. Dub top performers: YT custom audio (ElevenLabs) + Meta AI translation toggle.
7. Weekly: pull analytics → feed winners back as examples into the hook skill.

### 3e. Volume math to 100/day (illustrative)
- 3 long-form/week × ~15 clips = ~45 unique shorts/week. Plus a quarterly 100-short batch day = ~8/week.
- Each unique short goes natively to ~5 platforms (IG, TikTok, YT Shorts, FB, LinkedIn) with platform-specific copy/edits: **~265 video posts/week ≈ 38/day.**
- Plus text/carousels from the same transcripts: ~40–60/day across Threads, LinkedIn, X, FB, IG.
- Plus translated variants (Meta AI/YouTube dubs don't create new posts but add reach; separate ES account adds ~10/day).
- **≈ 100/day without new filming.** 1,000/day needs more **distinct source footage** (interviews, lives, guest clips, client stories) and more accounts with **distinct content**, not duplicates (see risks).

### What keeps Andrew safe (operational rules for the engine)
1. **Every post starts from Andrew-owned footage** (or a guest with permission plus Collab post). Never clip HighLevel's or others' videos as the main content.
2. **One distinct edit per platform per account.** Vary at least: hook line/on-screen text, opening shot, layout (split vs full-face vs screen-only), caption style, length. Don't count on speed changes, mirroring or watermarks to make something "original". IG explicitly says they don't.
3. **No identical uploads across Andrew's multiple IG accounts** (@andrewpreneurship vs @aiandrewpreneurship). Give each account its own angle (GHL business vs AI tools) and its own cuts. Use **Collab posts** when one asset should hit both.
4. **Clean masters only**: no TikTok/CapCut watermarks on Reels/Shorts.
5. **Disclose realistic AI b-roll** (YouTube toggle, TikTok AI label, Meta "AI info"). Keep AI b-roll under ~20% of runtime as house style. Dubs are auto-labeled.
6. **Avoid template sameness on YouTube**: don't publish 50 near-identical "GHL tip #N" Shorts with the same background and VO structure. Rotate formats (inauthentic content = mass-produced or repetitive).
7. **Human review gate** before publish (Blotato drafts). Catches duplicates, wrong-platform copy and FTC issues (income claims, affiliate disclosure).
8. **Monitor monthly**: IG Account Status → recommendations eligibility; YouTube Studio monetization status. If IG flags you, post only fresh original content for 30 days.
