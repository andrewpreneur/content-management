## TL;DR (read this if nothing else)

1. **Position:** Andrew becomes **"the One Feature guy."** One-liner: *"Sell one HighLevel feature. Build a SaaS. I show you which one, who buys it and how to set it up."* A named framework is how Martell (DRIP Matrix), Hormozi (Grand Slam Offer, Content Accordion) and Liam Ottley ("AAA") became known for one thing.
2. **Signature series:** **"Sell This Feature"**: one GHL feature per episode, covering who buys it, what to charge and the build on screen. It runs weekly as long-form plus daily as shorts. Every episode ends with the SaaSSuite bridge (onboard · educate · support).
3. **The plan's biggest hole:** it has **no real pillar content**. Every brand studied here starts from a long piece of real footage (keynote, podcast, vlog, live build) and cuts it down. The current engine starts from 30–60s clips and *expands* them with AI long-form. The fix is **one real 45–60 min pillar a week**, plus the existing 2-hour shorts session.
4. **Benchmark on volume:** the biggest volume brands publish about **30/day on their main brand**, not 1,000. Team GaryVee's official page lists 30 people, 55 channels and 200 pieces/week ([Team GaryVee](https://garyvaynerchuk.com/team-garyvee/)). Hormozi said 35,000 pieces in 40 months, about 29/day ([recap](https://deciphr.ai/podcast/how-i-gained-78-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). So 1,000/day must come mostly from **language, niche and clipper surfaces**, and the main handles stay at quality level.
5. **Measure the brand, not the posts:** add follower growth, profile visits, email subscribers, inbound podcast and collab invites, and branded search ("Andrew George HighLevel") to the weekly report. Martell's creative director: *views don't matter if they don't convert to followers* ([Action Academy](https://theactionacademy.buzzsprout.com/1862491/episodes/18808766-how-sam-gaudet-grew-dan-martell-to-10-million-followers)).
6. **Top 3 expansion plays (best impact for the effort):** (1) podcast guesting on GHL/agency shows, 2/month; (2) his own SaaS-founder interview series as IG Collab posts; (3) a free "One Feature Playbook" lead magnet plus a snapshot to build an owned list. The clipper program and LevelUp 2027 stage come next.

### 2.3 Signature series to own

### 2.6 Gaps in the current plan (docs/) that block brand growth

1. **No real pillar content.** `multiplier.md` starts from 60–90s clips. Long-form is "half-screen AI Andrew" built *from* shorts. Every brand studied runs pillar → micro, not micro → pillar. → Add the weekly real long-form (§2.4) to `roadmap.md` and the film list template.
2. **The success metric is post count, not brand.** The roadmap's exit criteria are posts/day only. → Add weekly: follower growth per main handle, profile visits, email subscribers, inbound guest/collab invites, branded search, and SaaSSuite trials attributed to content.
3. **No positioning or category name in any doc.** Scripts say "Follow" with no reason to. → Put the One Feature positioning line and bio template into `docs/` and the Second Brain *Identity* doc.
4. **No person in the content.** Film list #1 is 5/5 feature tips. → Pillar 4 (building in public) needs ~15% of the film list and a daily 5-min capture habit.
5. **No owned audience.** Newsletter platform is undecided (Substack / beehiiv / GHL email all "To create"); there's no lead magnet; "SUITE" is the only keyword. → Pick one list (GHL email is fine), ship *The One Feature Playbook*, and give every pillar its own comment keyword.
6. **No outbound distribution.** `expansion.md` has one line on collabs and nothing on guesting, events or clippers. Everything else in the plan is posting to Andrew's own accounts. → Add §2.5 plays 1, 2, 6 and 7 to `expansion.md`.
7. **1,000/day versus brand quality.** Benchmarks: Team GaryVee 200/week across 55 channels with 30 people; Hormozi about 29/day. 85 accounts × 12 posts/day puts the main brand at risk (low-quality spillover, originality penalties, a template-farm look). → **Cap main handles** (IG/YT/LinkedIn/Threads/TikTok) at about 30–40 quality posts/day combined. The rest of the 1,000 goes to language/niche pages and clippers, and **every surface keeps the same name and look** so volume adds to *one* recognizable brand.
8. **Brand kit and voice gate not done.** Colors/fonts are TBD, and the Claude voice skill isn't built. A visual signature is a big part of recognition (Hormozi captions, Chris Do carousels). → Make it a 30-day must, not a nice-to-have.
9. **Handle sprawl.** andrewpreneurship / realandrewgeorge / aiandrewpreneurship / planned andrewpreneur-espanol etc., and TikTok handle unknown. → One naming pattern (`@andrewpreneurship`, `@andrewpreneurship.es`, `@andrewpreneurship.ai`?) before new pages are created. Decide before day 31.
10. **Platform dependence on HighLevel.** The brand, affiliate income and SaaSSuite all depend on one vendor, and the affiliate terms reportedly allow content reuse. → Position as "one feature" (portable to other platforms later) and own the email list.
11. **Translation approach unsettled.** `competitors.md` recommends main-channel multi-language audio first, while `expansion.md` plans separate pages, and the evidence conflicts (§2.5 #5). → A/B for 30 days.
12. **No collaboration with other people's footage.** Interviews and guesting are also the safest answer to IG's April 2026 originality rules: new faces mean original footage.
